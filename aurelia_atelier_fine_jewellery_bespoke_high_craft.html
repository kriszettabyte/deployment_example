The Atelier & Heritage

Where Architectural Rigor Meets Timeless Goldsmithing.

Founded as an intimate high-jewellery studio, AURELIA ATELIER rejects mass casting in favor of hand-forged metalwork. Every curve is drawn by hand, wax-carved under magnification, and micro-pavé set by master artisans dedicating up to 140 hours to a single creation.

100% Traceable, Conflict-Free Gemstones

Every diamond and precious sapphire is accompanied by a full origin dossier and GIA certification. We partner exclusively with single-mine, carbon-neutral lapidaries to guarantee ethical integrity from earth to heirloom.
